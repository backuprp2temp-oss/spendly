Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored only as a secure hash). To provide the core functionality of the Service, we also collect the expense data you choose to enter — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect payment card numbers, bank credentials, or access to your financial accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data solely to operate and improve the Service: displaying your expense summaries, generating category breakdowns and monthly reports, and maintaining your account. We use your email address only for essential communications such as account verification and important service notices. We do not sell your personal or financial data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on our servers in a database that is protected by reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. Expense records are retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us, after which your data will be permanently removed, except where retention is required by law.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third-party services to function — for example, web fonts loaded from Google Fonts when pages are rendered. These services may receive basic technical information such as your IP address in the course of delivery. We do not share your expense data or account information with any third party beyond what is strictly necessary to operate the Service, and we do not permit third parties to use your data for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, want to access, correct, or delete your data, or wish to raise a privacy concern, please reach out at privacy@spendly.app. We aim to respond to all inquiries within a reasonable timeframe.

Also see our Terms and Conditions.
